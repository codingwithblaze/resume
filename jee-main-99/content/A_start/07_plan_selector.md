## Step 2: hours → variant

| Honest daily average | Variant | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| up to 6.5 h | **6 h** | School-going Class 12 on weekdays; mocks on Sundays |
| 6.5 – 9 h | **8 h** | Most common; school + evening study, or dropper with coaching |
| 9 h or more | **10 h** | Dropper or holiday period. **Not more than 10 h**: beyond that, retention and sleep suffer |
| **School weekdays + free weekends** | **Hybrid**: 6 h Mon–Fri, 10 h Sat–Sun | Averages about 7.1 h/day. The plans mark which tasks move to the weekend |

:::warning Sleep isn't negotiable
Every variant keeps **8 hours of sleep** (7.5 minimum on the occasional late night). For 13–18-year-olds, the American Academy of Sleep Medicine recommends 8–10 hours [LS-AASM]. Memory consolidation happens during sleep: cutting sleep to add study hours reliably *reduces* what you retain.
:::

## Step 3: level → modifications

| Level | Modification |
|---|---|
| **Beginner** | Choose the *next longer* plan if your days allow. Otherwise drop Low-frequency topics (page [[pyq-matrix]]) until Phase 3 and double the Basic-question practice in Phase 1 |
| **Intermediate** | Use the plan as written |
| **Advanced** | Compress Phase 1 by 25%. Use the saved days for extra full mocks and Hard-question sets |

## Step 4: weak-subject weighting

Default split of *problem-solving time* is Physics 34% · Chemistry 30% · Maths 36%. Adjust by 10 percentage points towards your weakest subject:

| Weakest subject | Physics | Chemistry | Maths |
|---|---|---|---|
| none / balanced | 34% | 30% | 36% |
| **Maths** | 30% | 25% | **45%** |
| **Physics** | **44%** | 25% | 31% |
| **Chemistry** | 30% | **40%** | 30% |
