# How to Use This Book {#how-to-use}

## The visual code

Every box in this book has one job, and its colour and label tell you what that job is. After a week you will scan pages by colour.

:::formula Formula
Formulas and standard results you must be able to write from memory. Example: $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$.
:::

:::shortcut Shortcut
A legitimate faster method, with *when to use it* and *when not to use it*.
:::

:::important Important
A concept that questions are built on. If you skip this, later questions in the chapter stop making sense.
:::

:::trap Mistake Alert
A trap that has cost students marks: sign errors, unit slips, misread conditions, NCERT exceptions.
:::

:::pyq PYQ Pattern
How this idea is typically asked in JEE Main: the recurring question *structure*, not a copied question.
:::

:::revision Revision
What to re-read on Day 2, Day 7 and Day 30. Tick the boxes as you go.
:::

:::practice Practice
Original questions with answer, full solution, shortcut, concept tested, difficulty, target time and common trap.
:::

:::mock Mock Test
Timed test material and instructions: sit it like the real exam.
:::

:::warning Warning
Rules where a mistake costs you directly, such as negative marking or exam-day regulations.
:::

:::fact Verified Fact
Information taken from an official document (NTA, NCERT, JoSAA). Re-check it against the 2027 bulletin.
:::

:::ref Reference
Textbook reference data, such as reagent tables, trends and tests, collected for quick lookup.
:::

:::data Third-Party Data
Numbers from coaching or portal analyses, such as marks-vs-percentile ranges. Approximate by nature.
:::

:::strategy Strategy
A method or routine this book recommends: how to study, attempt or review.
:::

## Three kinds of statement, three labels

| Label | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| {{tag:verified}} | Taken from an official NTA/NCERT/JoSAA document or an official announcement reported by a reputable outlet | "Each wrong answer carries −1 mark." |
| {{tag:third}} | From published analyses by coaching institutes or education portals; useful but not official | "Current Electricity usually has 2–3 questions per shift." |
| {{tag:rec}} | This book's strategy advice, derived from the rules, the trend data and learning research | "Attempt Chemistry first if it is your fastest subject." |

## Difficulty and question tags

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| {{diff:E}} | Easy: direct formula or fact. Target: under 1.5 min. |
| {{diff:M}} | Medium: two steps, or one idea plus careful calculation. Target: 2–3 min. |
| {{diff:H}} | Hard: multi-concept, long algebra, or an unusual twist. Target: 3–5 min, attempt only in Round 2. |
| **NV** | Numerical-value answer (Section B style). There are no options to check against, so be careful: a wrong answer here also costs −1. |
| **Speed** | Time-pressure item: solve it in the target time or skip it. |
| **Tricky** | Contains a deliberate trap. Read the *Common trap* line after solving. |

## Navigating the PDF

:::grid2
**On a laptop or tablet**

- The **Contents** pages are clickable: tap any line to jump there.
- Every page has **quick-jump tabs** in the footer: *Contents · Physics · Chemistry · Maths · Formulas · Plans · Trackers*.
- Open your PDF reader's **bookmarks/outline panel** for a collapsible map of the whole book.
- "Page 123"-style references in the text are clickable too.
- The **progress bar** at the top of each page shows how far through the book you are, coloured by subject.
+++
**On paper**

- Everything is designed for A4. Print only the pages you need, when you need them.
- **Planner pages** (Part X), **trackers** (Part XIII) and the two-page **Mock Analysis Sheet** (page [[mock-analysis-sheet]]): print one set, or one per mock.
- **Revision pages:** print the Formula Handbook (Part V) and the Last-Minute sheets (Part XII) double-sided and keep them in your bag.
- The **Exam Day Command Sheet** (page [[command-sheet]]) is a single page. Print two copies: one for your desk, one for exam morning.
- Chapter modules print well in grayscale: every box also has a text label.
:::

:::note About interactivity, honestly
A standard PDF cannot play real animations reliably across devices, so this book doesn't try to. What it does have: clickable contents, internal links, bookmarks, quick-jump tabs, QR codes to official resources, and **fillable checkboxes and text fields**. These work in Adobe Acrobat Reader, Xodo, Foxit and most desktop browsers. On some phone viewers, fields may show but not save. In that case, print the trackers.
:::

## The daily loop this book is designed around

:::flow
Learn | Read the module's concept summary and formula box (40–60 min per sub-topic)
Practise | Solve the module's practice set, timed, then check solutions
Log | Every error goes into The Mistake Book with its type
Revise | Same-day 10-min recap → Day 2 → Day 7 → Day 14 → Day 30
Test | Chapter test → mixed tests → full mocks, analysed with the Mock Analysis Sheet
:::
