## Your timeline, in one line

:::fact Verified: JEE Main 2027 Session 1 dates
NTA's tentative examination calendar (public notice dated 16 September 2026) schedules **JEE Main 2027 Session 1 on 22, 23, 24, 28, 29 and 30 January 2027**, with 31 January as a buffer day. NTA has said these dates are tentative. The detailed Information Bulletin, with eligibility, pattern and syllabus confirmation, had **not** been released when this book was compiled (28 September 2026). Session 2 is expected in April 2027 but had not been announced. Sources: [NTA-CAL27], [NTA-WEB].
:::

Counting from 28 September 2026, you have about **116 days to Session 1** and about **185–190 days to an early-April Session 2**. That is why this book's **120-day plan** is built to peak in late January and its **180-day plan** carries you through to April. If you are reading this later, the Study-Plan Selector (page [[plan-selector]]) picks the right plan for the days you have left.

## Three promises this book makes, and one it doesn't

:::grid2
**What this book promises**

- **Accuracy over hype.** Exam rules are taken from NTA documents and labelled *Verified*. Trend numbers from coaching analyses are labelled *Third-party analysis*. Our own advice is labelled *Recommendation*.
- **Complete coverage.** Every unit of the official syllabus has a module. Topics that NTA removed in 2024 are flagged so you don't waste time on them.
- **Original material.** Every practice question here was written for this book. PYQs are referenced and classified, not copied.
+++
**What it will not promise**

No book, coach or strategy can *guarantee* a percentile, rank or college. Percentile depends on your raw score **and** on how everyone else in your shift performs. What we can do is show you, with historical ranges, what score band usually corresponds to 99 percentile, and then give you a system for reaching the top of that band with a safety margin.
:::
