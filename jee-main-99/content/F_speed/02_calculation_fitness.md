JEE Main allows no calculator (page [[exam-facts]]). Hand calculation is a trainable skill, like fitness: 15 minutes a day for eight weeks makes a visible difference to both speed and error rate {{tag:rec}}.

## Mental-arithmetic methods

:::shortcut Squares near a base
$(a \pm b)^2 = a^2 \pm 2ab + b^2$: $48^2 = (50 - 2)^2 = 2500 - 200 + 4 = 2304$. Numbers ending in 5: $n5^2 = n(n + 1)$ followed by 25, so $65^2 = 4225$.
:::

:::shortcut Products near 100
$97\times94$: the deficits are 3 and 6. $97 - 6 = 91$ and $3\times6 = 18$, so the product is 9118. This works for any pair near a power of 10.
:::

:::shortcut Percentages & ratios
$x\%$ of $y$ = $y\%$ of $x$: 16% of 25 = 25% of 16 = 4. Successive changes of $a\%$ and $b\%$ combine to $a + b + \dfrac{ab}{100}$ percent.
:::

:::shortcut Division by estimation
To divide by an awkward number, scale both parts first: $\dfrac{3.6}{0.012} = \dfrac{3600}{12} = 300$. Write powers of ten separately: $\dfrac{6\times10^{-7}}{3\times10^{-4}} = 2\times10^{-3}$.
:::

## The 15-minute daily drill {{tag:rec}}

:::flow
Minutes 0–4 · Tables | Recall one table from this page (squares, cubes, reciprocals, logs) from memory. Check it; redo the misses.
Minutes 4–10 · Timed set | 12 mixed calculations of the kind that appear in solutions: fraction simplification, powers of ten, a square root, a log. Write every step.
Minutes 10–13 · Algebra | 3 expansions or factorisations, such as $(a + b + c)^2$ or $x^3 - y^3$, or completing the square.
Minutes 13–15 · Log it | Record time and errors in the tracker below. Aim for fewer errors first, then speed.
:::

:::tip Rotate the content
Week 1–2: arithmetic only. Week 3–4: add logs, powers and roots. Week 5+: take the calculations from your own mock solutions (the arithmetic you actually got wrong). This makes the drill specific to you.
:::
