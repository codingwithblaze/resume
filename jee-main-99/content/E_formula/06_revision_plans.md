# Formula Revision Plans: 1-Day, 3-Day, 7-Day & Final 24 Hours {#formula-revision}

All four plans use the three-pass method (page [[formula-handbook]]): recall first, then check, then repeat what you missed. Reading a sheet without first trying to recall it feels productive, but it builds far less memory than testing yourself [LS-DUN], [LS-RK]. The plans are recommendations {{tag:rec}}; adjust them to your weak areas from the Mistake Book.

## The 7-Day Formula Cycle

Use this cycle once a month during preparation, and as the formula part of the final week.

| Day | Session 1 (60 min) | Session 2 (45 min) | Re-test (15 min) | Done |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Physics P01–P07 | Maths M01–M03 | yesterday's dots | [ ] |
| 2 | Physics P08–P12 | Maths M04–M06 | Day 1 dots | [ ] |
| 3 | Physics P13–P20 | Maths M07–M09 | Day 2 dots | [ ] |
| 4 | Chemistry C01–C08 (physical) | Maths M10 | Day 1 + Day 3 dots | [ ] |
| 5 | Chemistry C09–C12 (inorganic) + NCERT tables | Maths M11–M14 | Day 4 dots | [ ] |
| 6 | Chemistry C14–C20 (organic): reagent and named-reaction tables | Constants & frequently forgotten list | Day 2 + Day 5 dots | [ ] |
| 7 | Full self-test: 60 random formulas across all three subjects | Rewrite every missed formula 3 times | all remaining dots | [ ] |

## The 3-Day Formula Sprint

For the gap between two mocks, or when you have lost touch with the formulas after a break.

:::grid3
**Day 1 · Physics**

- [ ] Morning: sheets P01–P10 (recall, then check)
- [ ] Afternoon: sheets P11–P20
- [ ] Evening: frequently forgotten list (Physics); re-test the dots
+++
**Day 2 · Chemistry**

- [ ] Morning: physical sheets C01–C08
- [ ] Afternoon: inorganic trends, then the reagent and named-reaction tables
- [ ] Evening: distinguishing tests; re-test the Day 1 and Day 2 dots
+++
**Day 3 · Maths**

- [ ] Morning: M01–M07
- [ ] Afternoon: M08–M14, conic table from memory
- [ ] Evening: constants; the full dot list from all three days
:::

## The 1-Day Formula Blitz

For the day before a mock, or one revision day in the final fortnight. About 6 hours, with a 10-minute break every hour.

:::flow
Hour 1 · Physics mechanics | P02–P06 sheets. Write the vertical-circle, banking, rolling and orbit results from memory.
Hour 2 · Physics rest | P07–P20. Focus on thermodynamics processes, optics sign conventions and the AC table.
Hour 3 · Physical chemistry | C01–C08. Write out every formula with its units.
Hour 4 · Inorganic & organic | Trend exceptions, the reagent table, named reactions and tests. Say each reaction aloud.
Hour 5 · Maths | M01–M14. Conic table, definite-integral properties, determinant rules, P&C distribution.
Hour 6 · Frequently forgotten + dots | All three trap tables, then everything you dotted today. Stop by evening and sleep normally.
:::

## The Final 24 Hours

:::important The last 24 hours are for confidence and sleep, not new material
Research on sleep and memory consolidation shows that sleep after learning helps you retain it, and sleep deprivation impairs attention and working memory [LS-RB], [LS-AASM]. Do **not** study past your normal bedtime on the eve of the exam.
:::

| Time (for a morning shift) | Formula task |
|---|---|
| Eve, morning | 7-Day cycle "Day 7" self-test, but only 30 formulas per subject |
| Eve, afternoon | Frequently forgotten list; the personal dot list from the last 7 days |
| Eve, evening (max 60 min) | Read the Last-Minute Revision Book sheets (page [[last-minute]]) once, calmly. No new problems. |
| Exam morning | Optional 10-minute glance at your personal "top 20 forgotten" card. Nothing else. |
| In the hall | Before starting, write any 3–5 formulas you tend to forget on the rough sheet (only after the exam officially starts). |
