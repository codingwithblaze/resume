## The three-pass method {{tag:rec}}

:::flow
Pass 1 · Recall | Cover the right column of a sheet and write each formula from memory on a rough sheet. Retrieval practice is one of the best-supported learning techniques [LS-DUN], [LS-RK].
Pass 2 · Check & mark | Uncover and compare. Put a dot next to every formula you got wrong or hesitated on.
Pass 3 · Repeat the dots | Re-test only the dotted formulas the next day, then after 3 and 7 days (spaced revision, page [[mistake-book]]). A formula with three dots goes into the Frequently Forgotten list.
:::

:::important Conditions are part of the formula
Most formula errors in JEE come from using a correct formula outside its conditions: $v = u + at$ only for constant acceleration; $S_\infty$ only for $\lvert r\rvert < 1$; $\Delta G^\circ = -RT\ln K$ uses the standard-state $K$. The sheets list the condition next to each formula where it matters. Revise the condition along with the formula.
:::

:::tip Printing
The sheets are designed for A4 print at 100% scale. For a pocket version, print two pages per sheet (A5 each). Text stays readable down to about 70%.
:::
