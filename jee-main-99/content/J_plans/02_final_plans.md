# The Final Plans: 30, 14, 7 & 3 Days {#final-plans}

The last month decides how much of your preparation turns into marks. The principles below hold for every final plan {{tag:rec}}:

:::grid2
**Do**

- Take mocks **in your exam time slot** (9:00 or 15:00) so your body is alert at that hour.
- Revise from **your own material**: the Formula Handbook, the Mistake Book, the Last-Minute sheets.
- Keep the sleep schedule fixed. Wake at the time you'll wake on exam day.
- Keep exercise and meals normal.
+++
**Don't**

- Start new chapters or new books in the last 14 days.
- Take more than one mock a day, or a mock on the exam eve.
- Compare mock scores with friends. Different series have different difficulty.
- Study past your bedtime. Sleep consolidates memory [LS-RB], and tiredness costs more marks than one extra hour gains.
:::

**Using the tables:** blocks are about 2 hours. **6 h** = Blocks 1–3 · **8 h** = Blocks 1–4 · **10 h** = Blocks 1–5 (page [[long-plans]]). On mock days, the mock and its analysis fill Blocks 1–3 for every variant.

:::important Why the last mock is on D−3
A mock two days before the exam gives you a final rehearsal and still leaves a full recovery day. A bad score on D−3 changes nothing about your preparation: treat it only as a reminder of the traps to watch for.
:::

# Final 3 Days {#final-3}

A detailed version of D−3 to D−1 for a **9:00 shift** (shift everything by 6 hours for a 15:00 shift).

| Time | D−3 | D−2 | D−1 (eve) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6:00 | wake, water, 15-min walk | wake, walk | wake, walk |
| 7:00 | breakfast (exam-day menu) | breakfast | breakfast |
| 8:00–9:00 | Last-Minute sheet (one subject) | Formula Handbook pass 1 | Last-Minute sheets, one calm read |
| 9:00–12:00 | **Final mock** | 15 easy questions per subject, untimed | light: the Command Sheet (page [[command-sheet]]), the Frequently Forgotten list |
| 12:00–14:00 | lunch, rest | Mistake Book repeats | lunch; pack the exam bag |
| 14:00–16:00 | check wrong answers only | Formula Handbook pass 2 | walk, rest, **no new problems** |
| 16:00–18:00 | 20 easy Maths questions | documents, route, centre visit if possible | family time, light reading |
| 18:00–19:00 | exercise | exercise | light stretching |
| 19:00–21:00 | dinner, Formula pass (one subject) | dinner, Formula pass 3 | dinner by 20:00; 20 min of formulas at most |
| 21:30 | screens off | screens off | screens off by 21:00 |
| 22:00 | sleep | sleep | sleep by 22:00 |
