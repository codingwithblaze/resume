# The Weekly System & Scorecard {#weekly-system}

A week is the natural unit of preparation: long enough to finish chapters, short enough to correct course. Each week follows the same rhythm, and ends with a 45-minute review that decides the next week {{tag:rec}}.

## The weekly rhythm

| Day | Main work | Evening |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | New chapters (all three subjects) | R0 + R1 |
| Tuesday | New chapters | R0 + R1 |
| Wednesday | New chapters + one timed mixed set | R0 + R2 |
| Thursday | New chapters | R0 + R1 |
| Friday | New chapters | R0 + R2 |
| Saturday | Chapter tests of the week; PYQs; backlog | R3 items due |
| Sunday | Morning: test or mock (exam slot) · afternoon: analysis · evening: **weekly review** | light; early night |

:::tip The half-day of rest
Take one half-day off each week: Sunday evening after the review, or Wednesday evening. Decide it in advance, so the rest doesn't make you feel guilty.
:::

## The 45-minute weekly review

:::flow
1 · Score the week (10 min) | Fill in the scorecard below honestly.
2 · Mistake Book count (10 min) | Count this week's entries by type (page [[mistake-book]]). The largest type gets a specific fix next week.
3 · Plan check (10 min) | Compare the plan's week with what you actually did. Move any backlog into named slots; don't just "catch up later".
4 · Next week (15 min) | Write each day's three blocks with specific outputs. Put the three mock action items into the first half of the week.
:::

## Weekly Scorecard

Score each line 0, 1 or 2 against its target. 16 or more is a strong week, 12–15 a normal week. Below 12 for two weeks in a row: re-check the plan's hours (page [[plan-selector]]) and the warning signs of burnout (page [[psychology]]).
