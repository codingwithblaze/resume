# The Final Checklist {#final-checklist}

Work through this list in the last 14 days. Every item should be ticked before exam day. Any unticked item goes into the Final 7 Days plan (page [[final-7]]).

:::grid2
**Content readiness**

[ ] Every P1 (Must-do) topic studied and its chapter test taken (page [[pyq-matrix]])
[ ] Chapter-wise PYQs 2024–2026 done for all P1 and P2 chapters (page [[pyq-tracker]])
[ ] At least two full 7-Day Formula Cycles completed (page [[formula-revision]])
[ ] Blank-sheet formula test ≥ 90% in each subject
[ ] NCERT inorganic tables and exceptions read twice (page [[ncert-strategy]])
[ ] Organic reagent and named-reaction tables recalled without notes
[ ] Every Mistake Book **repeat** re-solved correctly
[ ] Last-Minute sheets read once (page [[last-minute]])
+++
**Exam readiness**

[ ] At least 8 full mocks analysed with the Mock Analysis Sheet
[ ] Last 5 mocks: in the target band, with ≤ 8 negative marks
[ ] Subject order and checkpoints tested in ≥ 5 mocks and fixed
[ ] Round system and 3-minute rule used without thinking
[ ] At least 3 mocks taken in the exact exam slot
[ ] Command Sheet filled in with personal traps (page [[command-sheet]])
[ ] Panic reset practised in at least two mocks
[ ] Exam-day breakfast and routine rehearsed on mock days
:::

:::grid2
**Logistics**

[ ] Admit card downloaded; name, photo, date, shift and centre checked
[ ] Valid photo ID and photographs as specified on the admit card
[ ] Any required declaration or undertaking filled in
[ ] Centre route and travel time known; travel companion arranged
[ ] Clothing that follows the admit-card guidelines ready
[ ] Two alarms set; phone plan for the journey
+++
**Body and mind**

[ ] Sleep at a fixed time for the last 7 nights (8 hours)
[ ] Wake-up time matched to the exam slot for 7 days
[ ] Exercise or a walk every day
[ ] Screens off an hour before sleep
[ ] Worries written down before the last mock and on the exam morning (page [[psychology]])
[ ] Reminder: a hard paper is hard for the whole shift. Normalisation handles it
:::

:::important After Session 1
Take two days' rest, then follow the Session 1 → Session 2 bridge (page [[plan-180]]). Only the better of your two NTA scores is used (page [[exam-facts]]), so Session 2 is a genuine second chance.
:::
