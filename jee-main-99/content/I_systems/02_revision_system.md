# The Revision System {#revision-system}

Forgetting is predictable: without review, much of what you learn today fades within days. Two of the best-supported findings in learning research give the remedy. **Spaced** review, spread over increasing intervals, beats the same time spent in one block [LS-CEP]. **Retrieval** (testing yourself) beats re-reading [LS-RK], [LS-DUN]. This system builds both into a fixed schedule, so revision never depends on how you feel that day.

## The six revision points

| Point | When | Time per chapter | What you do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R0 · Same day** | the evening you study it | 15–20 min | Blank-page recall: write the chapter's key formulas and ideas from memory, then check. Solve 5 quick questions. |
| **R1 · Day 2** | two days later | 15 min | Formula recall (cover-and-write) and re-solve the day's wrong questions. |
| **R2 · Day 7** | one week later | 30–45 min | A mixed set of 15 questions from the chapter, timed. Log errors in the Mistake Book. |
| **R3 · Day 14** | two weeks later | 30 min | Chapter-wise PYQs (2024–2026), about 15 questions. |
| **R4 · Day 30 / monthly** | once a month | 45–60 min | The chapter test from the module, the formula sheet (page [[formula-handbook]]) and the Mistake Book entries for the chapter. |
| **R5 · Final** | last 30 days | per the final plans | Formula Handbook cycles, Last-Minute sheets, repeats from the Mistake Book (page [[final-plans]]). |

:::important Budget {{tag:rec}}
Revision should take about **20–25% of daily study time** during the learning phase, and most of it in the final phase. If revision is regularly squeezed out, you'll relearn chapters later at a much higher cost.
:::

:::tip Interleave once the chapters pile up
After about six chapters, make R2 and R3 sets **mixed**: questions from several chapters in random order. Mixed practice is harder in the moment, but trains you to choose the method, which is what the exam tests [LS-ROH].
:::

## A normal day with revision built in

:::flow
Morning block | New learning (the chapter of the day).
Afternoon block | Practice on the new chapter, then R1 for the chapter from two days ago.
Evening (45–60 min) | R0 for today's chapter, R2 for the chapter from a week ago, Mistake Book re-attempts due today.
Sunday | Weekly review: R3/R4 items due, the Mistake Book type count, next week's plan (page [[weekly-system]]).
:::
