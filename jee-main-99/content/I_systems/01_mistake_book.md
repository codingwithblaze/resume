# The Mistake Book {#mistake-book}

Most marks between 95 and 99+ percentile are not lost to topics you don't know. They are lost to **repeated, preventable mistakes** in topics you do know. The Mistake Book is a system for making each mistake once only.

:::important The one rule
Every wrong or "knew-it-but-skipped" question from practice, PYQs and mocks gets **one entry**, classified into **one of eight types**. Each type has its own prevention routine. Re-attempt the entry after 7 days. If you get it wrong again, it becomes a **repeat**: the highest priority in your weekly review.
:::

## The eight mistake types

:::grid2
**1 · CG · Concept gap**
You didn't understand the idea, or you applied the wrong principle.
*Example:* using energy conservation in an inelastic collision.

**Prevention:** re-learn the concept from the module and NCERT. Explain it aloud in 60 seconds without notes. Then solve 5 **fresh** questions on the same model. Reading the solution is not enough.
+++
**2 · FR · Formula or fact recall**
You knew the topic but misremembered a formula, condition, exception or NCERT fact.
*Example:* $\dfrac{4T}{r}$ used for a drop instead of a bubble.

**Prevention:** add it to your personal "forgotten" card. Test it with the three-pass method (page [[formula-handbook]]) on days 1, 3 and 7. Write the **condition** next to the formula.
:::

:::grid2
**3 · CA · Calculation**
The method was right; the arithmetic or algebra went wrong.
*Example:* $\tfrac{3}{5}\times\tfrac{10}{9} = \tfrac{3}{2}$ (wrong; it is $\tfrac23$).

**Prevention:** write every step in a boxed area of the rough sheet. Cancel before multiplying. Verify NV answers by substitution or a quick estimate. Do the 15-minute daily drill (page [[speed-drills]]).
+++
**4 · MR · Misreading**
You solved a different question from the one asked: missed "not", "incorrect" or "except"; wrong unit; radius instead of diameter; $d$ instead of $d^2$.

**Prevention:** underline the **asked quantity** and any negative word before solving. Write "Find: ___" at the top of your rough-sheet box. Re-read the last line of the question before you mark the answer.
:::

:::grid2
**5 · TR · Transcription & clicking**
The right answer in your rough work, but a wrong value copied, a wrong option clicked, or a digit dropped while typing.

**Prevention:** number your rough-sheet boxes by question. Before saving, compare the screen with the rough sheet: "option letter, value, sign". In the NTA interface, check the palette colour after saving.
+++
**6 · TS · Time & selection**
You spent too long on one question, or ran out of time with easy questions unread.

**Prevention:** the 3-minute rule; the round system and checkpoint times (page [[hall-protocol]]); a quick first scan of each section. Track the number of UK questions per mock. It should fall week by week.
:::

:::grid2
**7 · GU · Guessing (over-attempt)**
You answered with fewer than two options eliminated, or entered an NV answer you hadn't finished.

**Prevention:** apply the two-elimination rule (page [[answer-choice]]). In every mock, mark each guess with "?" on the rough sheet and count how many were right. Most students find their guesses score below break-even.
+++
**8 · PR · Pressure**
Mistakes you would never make in calm practice: blanking, panic, careless errors late in the paper.

**Prevention:** rehearse under exam conditions at exam time; sleep 8 hours; use the reset routine (page [[psychology]]). Just before the test, write briefly about your worries (shown to improve exam performance in anxious students [LS-RAM]). Treat the adrenaline as fuel, not as a threat [LS-JAM].
:::

## How to keep the book

:::flow
1 · Log within 24 hours | Use the entry format below, in a notebook or on the printable pages. One line of diagnosis is enough. Accuracy matters more than neatness.
2 · Tag the type | One of the eight codes. If two apply, choose the **first** thing that went wrong.
3 · Write the fix | A specific action ("revise lens sign convention; solve 5 lens questions"), not a feeling ("be careful").
4 · Re-attempt on day 7 | Cover the solution and re-solve. Right: tick it. Wrong: mark **R** (repeat) and schedule it again for day 14.
5 · Weekly review (30 min) | Count entries by type. The most frequent type decides next week's focus (e.g. many CA entries: daily drill plus verification habit).
:::

:::tip What a good entry looks like
**P-13 · Mock 4 Q17 · MR** · Asked for the field at $2R$ *inside* a hollow sphere (answer 0). I used the outside formula. **Fix:** draw the region before choosing a formula. Re-attempt on 14 Mar [ ] ✓ / R
:::
