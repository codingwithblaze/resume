# Repeated Question Structures {#pyq-structures}

These are the question structures that recur most visibly in 2024–2026 papers, classified from third-party PYQ archives {{tag:third}} [T-EXAMSIDE], [T-MATHONGO]. They are described here in the book's own words, not reproduced. Each has original practice questions in its module. Once you can spot these structures, a large share of every paper is familiar before you start writing.
