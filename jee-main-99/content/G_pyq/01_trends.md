# PYQ Trend Analysis 2024–2026 {#pyq-trends}

:::warning How to read this part
NTA does **not** publish chapter-wise statistics. Every weight in this part comes from **third-party analyses** of the 2024–2026 papers (coaching institutes and PYQ archives), compiled into ranges {{tag:third}} [T-WT-VED], [T-WT-CD], [T-PHY26], [T-ANALYSIS26]. The ranges are approximate: individual shifts vary, and different analysts group topics differently. The priorities built on them are this book's recommendations {{tag:rec}}. The 2024 syllabus revision [NTA-SYL] makes 2024–2026 the only fully comparable years, so older papers are used for practice only (page [[pyq-system]]).
:::

## What the 2024–2026 papers show

:::grid3
**Physics**

- Mostly **one principle plus a clean calculation**. A minority of each paper is lengthy.
- Modern Physics, Electronic Devices, EM Waves and Units & Errors form a fast-scoring **easy block** of 5–8 questions (page [[physics-strategy]]).
- Electrostatics, Magnetism, Current Electricity, Optics and Solids & Liquids carry the most weight.
- Experimental Skills (vernier, screw gauge, meter bridge) appear as direct questions in some shifts.
+++
**Chemistry**

- Repeatedly described as the section where **NCERT-strong students gain their edge** [T-ANALYSIS26].
- In 2026 counts, **Coordination Compounds (36)** and **Aldehydes, Ketones & Acids (35)** were the largest chapters; GOC and Kinetics had 27 each [T-WT-CD].
- Inorganic questions often come almost directly from NCERT text and tables.
- Physical Chemistry numericals are mostly single-step with simple numbers.
+++
**Mathematics**

- Repeatedly described as the **lengthiest section and the rank decider** [T-ANALYSIS26].
- Coordinate geometry, calculus and algebra (complex numbers, matrices, sequences) dominate.
- In 2026 counts, **Sequences & Series** and **Matrices & Determinants** had 33 each, 3D 29 and Vectors 27 [T-WT-CD].
- Many questions combine two units (e.g. a conic with a definite integral).
:::

## Five patterns that hold across 2024–2026 {{tag:rec}}

:::flow
1 · Standard models repeat | The same question structures (listed chapter by chapter in the "Recurring structures" boxes and on page [[pyq-structures]]) return every session with new numbers. Recognising a model is the fastest route to marks.
2 · Shifts differ in difficulty | Some shifts are clearly harder than others. Normalisation (page [[exam-facts]]) compensates, so a hard paper is not a disaster. Don't let a difficult start change your plan.
3 · NCERT is the base in Chemistry | Statements, exceptions, colours and tables from NCERT recur. The NCERT strategy (page [[ncert-strategy]]) is non-negotiable.
4 · Maths rewards selection | Because Maths is lengthy, the order of attempting (easy models first) matters more than in the other two subjects.
5 · Numerical questions are compulsory | Since 2025, all five Section B questions in each subject count, with negative marking. Numerical fluency is now worth up to 60 marks.
:::

## Topic-level shifts after the 2024 revision

:::fact Removed in 2024 (don't study for JEE Main) [NTA-SYL], [T-DELETED]
**Physics:** Communication Systems; several details inside units, such as some experimental topics. **Chemistry:** States of Matter, Surface Chemistry, Hydrogen, s-Block, Metallurgy, Environmental Chemistry, Polymers, Chemistry in Everyday Life. **Maths:** Mathematical Induction, Mathematical Reasoning, Heights & Distances; several subtopics inside units. Each module's syllabus note lists what is and isn't in the official text.
:::

:::note Old books and old papers
Pre-2024 question banks still contain questions on removed topics. When you use them, skip those questions rather than spending time learning removed material. The one deliberate exception in this book is the two-hour plane-geometry insurance in 3D (page [[m11]]).
:::
