# The PYQ Priority Matrix {#pyq-matrix}

The matrix ranks **topics** (finer than units) by four factors: how often they appear, how hard they are, how much study time they need, and how quickly their knowledge fades. It is **expert-derived** {{tag:rec}}: built from the unit ranges on page [[pyq-trends]] and from the question models in each module. It is not official data.

:::note Key
**Frequency:** Very high (in almost every shift) · High (most shifts) · Medium (about half the shifts) · Low (occasional).
**Priority:** P1 Must-do · P2 High · P3 Medium · P4 Low.
**Revision frequency:** how often to revisit the topic once it has been learned, up to the final month (spaced revision, page [[revision-system]]).
:::

## Priority lists by tier {{tag:rec}}

:::grid2
**P1 · Must-do** (learn to exam level, revise weekly or fortnightly)

**Physics:** errors; rotation; gravitation; fluids; thermodynamics; KTG; electrostatics & capacitors; circuits; magnetic effects; ray & wave optics; modern physics (dual nature, atoms, nuclei); semiconductors; EM waves.
**Chemistry:** atomic structure; bonding; thermodynamics; equilibrium; electrochemistry; kinetics; periodicity; p-block (15–17); d-block; coordination; GOC & isomerism; alcohols to acids; biomolecules.
**Maths:** complex numbers; quadratics; matrices & determinants; P&C; binomial; sequences; limits and derivatives; definite integrals; DE; circles and conics; 3D; vectors; statistics & probability.
+++
**P2 · High** (learn fully, revise every 2–4 weeks)

**Physics:** kinematics; laws of motion; work–energy; centre of mass; SHM & waves; EMI & AC; elasticity; heat transfer; potentiometer; experimental skills.
**Chemistry:** mole concept; solutions; conductance; groups 13–14 and 18; lanthanoids; purification; hydrocarbons; haloalkanes; amines; practical chemistry.
**Maths:** sets, relations and functions; indefinite integration; area; straight lines; trigonometry and inverse trigonometry.
:::

:::grid2
**P3 · Medium** (core ideas only, then PYQs)

**Physics:** magnetic materials (classification, Curie's law, $B$–$H$ basics); polarisation beyond Malus and Brewster.
**Chemistry:** isolated NCERT facts outside the main tables (e.g. uses of individual noble gases).
**Maths:** mean deviation for grouped data; unusual function types (greatest-integer compositions).
+++
**P4 · Low** (only after everything above is secure)

**Physics:** lengthy multi-body rotation problems (solve 2–3 per week for exposure, don't chase them).
**Chemistry:** detailed actinoid chemistry; rare named reactions not in NCERT.
**Maths:** planes in 3D beyond the insurance box (page [[m11]]); general solutions of complicated trigonometric equations.
:::
