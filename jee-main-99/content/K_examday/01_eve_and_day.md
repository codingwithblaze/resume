# The Exam Eve {#exam-eve}

The goal of the exam eve is simple: **arrive tomorrow rested, calm and on time.** Nothing you study today will add as many marks as a good night's sleep protects {{tag:rec}}.

:::important Three rules for the eve
1. **No new problems and no mock.** A hard question tonight can only shake your confidence.
2. **Revise your own sheets only:** the Command Sheet, the Frequently Forgotten list and one pass of the Last-Minute sheets.
3. **Screens off by 21:00, asleep by 22:00** (for a 9:00 shift).
:::

| Time | What to do | Done |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Wake at exam-day time. Walk. One calm pass of the Last-Minute sheets (page [[last-minute]]) | [ ] |
| Late morning | Re-read your Mistake Book's **repeat** entries only | [ ] |
| Afternoon | Rest, light activity, family time. Pack the exam bag (checklist below) | [ ] |
| 17:00 | Read the Command Sheet (page [[command-sheet]]) once, then put it in the bag | [ ] |
| 18:00 | Light exercise or a walk | [ ] |
| 19:30–20:00 | Early, familiar dinner. Nothing new or heavy | [ ] |
| 20:00–20:30 | Optional 20 minutes: the Frequently Forgotten list. Then close the books | [ ] |
| 21:00 | Screens off. Shower, light reading, set two alarms | [ ] |
| 22:00 | Lights out | [ ] |

:::revision Exam bag checklist (check against your admit card, which overrides this list)
[ ] Admit card (printed, all pages as instructed)  [ ] Original valid photo ID  [ ] Passport-size photographs as specified  [ ] Any declaration/undertaking the admit card requires  [ ] Transparent water bottle (if permitted)  [ ] Simple clothes that meet the dress guidelines, and no metal items  [ ] Money and a charged phone for the journey (to be left outside/as instructed)  [ ] Route and travel arranged; leave with a margin
:::

:::tip If you can't fall asleep
Don't check the time or your phone. Lie still with your eyes closed and breathe slowly (4 counts in, 6 out). Quiet rest still helps, and one imperfect night rarely decides an exam. Adrenaline will carry you through the morning.
:::

# The Exam-Day Plan {#exam-day-plan}

For a **9:00 shift** (shift everything by 6 hours for a 15:00 shift). Reporting and gate-closing times are printed on your admit card. In 2026, gates closed 30 minutes before the exam started [NTA-IB26].

| Time | Step | Done |
|---|---|---|
| 5:45 | Wake (your fixed mock-day time). Water, 5 minutes of stretching | [ ] |
| 6:00–6:30 | Familiar light breakfast (e.g. idli, poha or toast with eggs or paneer) and water. Nothing new | [ ] |
| 6:30 | Glance at your personal "top 5 forgotten formulas" card for 10 minutes at most | [ ] |
| Leave home | With a margin for traffic, so that you reach the centre well before reporting opens | [ ] |
| At the centre | Follow staff instructions; identity check and frisking. Don't discuss topics or predictions with other candidates | [ ] |
| Seated | Check your details on screen. Breathe: 4 in, 6 out, five times. Recall the first 5 minutes of the protocol (page [[hall-protocol]]) | [ ] |
| 9:00 | Start. Execute the plan you have practised in every mock | [ ] |
| After the exam | Don't analyse the paper with friends on the way out. Eat, rest, and switch off for the evening | [ ] |

:::important Between shifts and sessions
If you have taken Session 1 and plan to take Session 2, use the bridge plan (page [[plan-180]]). Analyse your response sheet only when NTA publishes the provisional key, not from memory on the day.
:::
